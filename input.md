# 1.0 TEST POLICY  {#test-policy .unnumbered}

This section explains the main philosophy of StayUniKL regarding
testing, including terms, definitions, and practices. Most of these
principles are based on ISTQB standards and are widely applied in
software testing across organizations, including educational
institutions like UniKL MIIT.

## 1.1 Software Testing Term Definition  {#software-testing-term-definition .unnumbered}

> Software testing in the StayUniKL system refers to the systematic
> process of evaluating and verifying that the system functions
> according to specified requirements. It involves executing the system
> to identify defects, ensure quality, and validate that all features
> such as court booking, user authentication, and notification services
> operate correctly.
>
> Testing ensures that the StayUniKL system is reliable, secure, and
> ready for deployment for UniKL students and administrators.
>
> In software testing, two important concepts are:

-   **Verification**:\
    The process of evaluating system components during development to
    ensure that they meet the specified requirements.\
    Example: Checking whether the booking module is developed according
    to the system design and SRS.

-   **Validation**:\
    The process of evaluating the final system to ensure that it meets
    user needs and intended use.\
    Example: Ensuring students can successfully book courts and receive
    correct notifications.

> Both verification and validation are essential to ensure that the
> StayUniKL system is built correctly and fulfils its intended purpose.

## 1.3 Testing Principles  {#testing-principles .unnumbered}

> • **Principle 1: Testing shows the presence of defects:**\
> Testing is used to identify defects within the StayUniKL system such
> as booking conflicts, incorrect user data handling, or notification
> delays. However, it does not guarantee that the system is completely
> free from errors.
>
> • **Principle 2: Exhaustive testing is impossible:**\
> It is not practical to test every possible combination of user actions
> and system conditions in StayUniKL. Therefore, testing is prioritised
> based on risk, focusing on core functionalities such as user
> registration, court booking, and notification management.
>
> • **Principle 3: Early testing:**\
> Testing activities are carried out as early as possible during the
> development of StayUniKL, including requirement validation and
> interface checking. This helps to detect issues before full system
> implementation, reducing rework cost and effort.
>
> • **Principle 4: Defect clustering:**\
> Defects in StayUniKL are likely to be concentrated in specific
> modules, particularly the booking system, authentication process, and
> notification features. Testing efforts are concentrated on these
> high-risk areas to improve efficiency.
>
> • **Principle 5: Pesticide paradox:**\
> If the same test cases are repeatedly executed in StayUniKL, they may
> no longer be effective in discovering new issues. Therefore, test
> cases are regularly updated and improved to ensure better defect
> detection.
>
> • **Principle 6: Testing is context-dependent:**\
> The testing approach for StayUniKL is designed based on its purpose as
> a student facility booking system. Focus is given to usability, system
> functionality, and performance under normal user conditions.
>
> • **Principle 7: Absence of errors fallacy:**\
> Even if StayUniKL operates without visible defects, it may still fail
> if it does not satisfy user needs such as smooth booking experience,
> clear notifications, and user-friendly design.
>
> .

## 1.4 Test Mission & Objectives  {#test-mission-objectives .unnumbered}

> The mission of **StayUniKL** testing is to ensure that the system
> delivers high quality performance, fulfils all functional
> requirements, and reduces the presence of defects. Testing activities
> are carried out in a structured manner to validate system
> functionality, usability, and overall reliability.
>
> Objectives include:\
> • Ensuring that all modules in StayUniKL function correctly for
> students and administrators, particularly in areas such as user
> registration, court booking, and notification management.
>
> • Identifying and documenting defects at early stages of testing to
> allow prompt correction and improvement of the system.
>
> • Verifying that the system operates reliably and efficiently under
> normal usage conditions while providing a smooth and user-friendly
> experience for all users.

## 1.5 Communication Among Stakeholders  {#communication-among-stakeholders .unnumbered}

> Testing in **StayUniKL** is a collaborative process that involves
> continuous communication between all relevant stakeholders, including
> developers, students, and system administrators. Effective
> communication is important to ensure that requirements, issues, and
> feedback are clearly understood and properly addressed.
>
> Professional and structured communication is maintained throughout the
> testing process to ensure that defects or system issues are reported
> accurately, analysed properly, and resolved in a timely manner.
> Feedback from users such as students is also considered important in
> improving system usability and overall performance.

## 1.6 SDLC Identification  {#sdlc-identification .unnumbered}

> **StayUniKL** adopts the **Prototyping Model** as its Software
> Development Life Cycle (SDLC) approach. This model is suitable because
> some system requirements may evolve during development, especially
> based on user feedback from students and administrators.
>
> The use of prototyping allows early versions of the system to be
> developed and tested, enabling continuous feedback collection and
> requirement refinement. Testing activities are integrated throughout
> the development process, allowing defects to be identified early and
> improvements to be made progressively.
>
> Through iterative cycles of development, testing, and enhancement,
> StayUniKL is continuously refined based on user interaction and
> feedback. This evolutionary approach ensures that the final system is
> aligned with user needs, functional requirements, and provides a
> smooth booking experience for students and administrators.
>

# 2.0 INTRODUCTION TO TEST PLAN  {#introduction-to-test-plan .unnumbered}

The purpose of this Software Testing Report (STR) is to document the
testing activities carried out for the **StayUniKL** system, a web-based
application developed to enhance student facility management at
Universiti Kuala Lumpur (UniKL). This project is developed as part of a
final year project to improve the efficiency of booking and managing
campus facilities such as sports courts.

The objective of this report is to define the test approach, test scope,
test case specifications, and validation methods used to ensure that the
StayUniKL system operates correctly and meets all requirements stated in
the Software Requirements Specification (SRS). It also serves as a
structured documentation of how testing is planned and executed.

The main goal of the testing process is to ensure that the system passes
the system testing phase successfully. StayUniKL consists of multiple
integrated modules, including user management, booking management, and
notification handling, which are used by different user roles such as
students and administrators. Therefore, it is important to ensure that
all components work together seamlessly as a complete system.

This report is organised to provide clear guidance on the system
overview, testing methodology, and documentation structure, ensuring
alignment with the Software Testing Plan (STP) requirements and academic
standards.

.

## 2.1 System Description  {#system-description .unnumbered}

> **StayUniKL** is a web-based facility booking management system
> designed to streamline the process of reserving and managing campus
> facilities for Universiti Kuala Lumpur (UniKL) students. The system
> primarily supports students and administrators by providing a
> centralized platform for booking sports courts and managing related
> activities.
>
> For students, StayUniKL provides modules that allow them to register
> and manage their user profiles, view available court schedules, make
> booking requests, and receive booking status updates through an
> integrated notification system. These features enable students to
> conveniently manage their facility reservations in a single platform
> without manual processes.
>
> For administrators, StayUniKL includes modules to manage user
> accounts, approve or reject booking requests, oversee court
> availability, and monitor overall system usage. Administrators are
> responsible for ensuring that booking operations run smoothly and that
> facility usage is properly controlled and organised.
>
> The system also includes a notification module that supports
> communication between users and administrators. This ensures that
> students are updated on booking statuses such as pending, approved, or
> rejected requests, improving transparency and user experience.
>
> By integrating all these modules into a single platform, StayUniKL
> ensures efficient data flow, proper access control for different user
> roles, and a more organised and automated facility booking process
> within UniKL.
>
> .
